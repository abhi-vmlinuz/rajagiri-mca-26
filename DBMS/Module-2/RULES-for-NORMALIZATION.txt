1NF - No multivalue in relation
2NF - LHS of the FD should be a proper subset of the candidate key and RHS should be a non prime attribute
3NF - LHS should be a super key  / candidate or RHS should be a prime attribute
BCNF - LHS should be a candidate / super key

=== Decomposition ====

Splitting the tables into smaller tables while preserving the data and ensuring mininal redundancy

types of composition: 
- loselesss decomposition : 1) Data isn't lost when decomposed : 2) Original relation can be reconstructed
- lossy decomposition : 1) Data may get lost : 2) original table cannot be fully reconstructed
- dependedncy preserving decomposition : ensures all functional dependencies are maintained
