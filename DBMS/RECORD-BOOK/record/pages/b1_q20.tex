{\fontsize{14}{17}\selectfont\textbf{20.}}\ Categorise every book using a CASE expression: price below 400 is BUDGET, price from 400 to 799 is STANDARD and price 800 or above is PREMIUM. Display book title, price and the computed price category, sorted by price descending.\par
\endgroup\vspace{12pt}

\codeheading{CODE:}

\begin{sqlcode}
SELECT title, price,
       CAST(CASE WHEN price < 400 THEN 'BUDGET'
                 WHEN price BETWEEN 400 AND 799 THEN 'STANDARD'
                 WHEN price >= 800 THEN 'PREMIUM'
            END AS VARCHAR2(14)) AS price_category
FROM book
ORDER BY price DESC;
\end{sqlcode}
